PYWEG · STORE EDITION

Privacy notice

This notice describes the PyWeg store edition currently in preparation. The browser demo with Darian and Nello profiles is a separate version.

Data stored on your device

Without an account, the app stores your learning progress, the most recently opened lesson, and your optional display name and avatar locally on your device. This data is not automatically sent to PyWeg or a server and is not synced between devices.

Python code and course answers

The app does not run Python code you enter. Answers and entries in the lessons are processed locally in your browser; they are not sent to a server.

Feedback and contact

Use “Send feedback” to write a suggestion, report a problem, or send another message. The app opens an email draft addressed to darian.sepe@student.hnu.de; it does not send anything automatically. Only if you send the draft from your email app will your email address and message text be transmitted to us through your email provider and used to handle your request. Your display name, avatar, and learning progress are not attached. You can edit or discard the draft before sending. You can also email us directly with questions.

Deleting your data

You can delete your learning progress from “Profile” by choosing “Reset progress”. Your display name and avatar are kept. To remove those details as well, clear the app data in Android system settings or uninstall the app.

Third parties and advertising

The prepared store edition does not include analytics or advertising services. Its course files and fonts are bundled locally in the app; the learning content does not require a connection to a third party.

Data controller and questions

PyWeg is provided by Darian Sepe, Angelweg 14, 72669 Unterensingen, Germany. For questions about the app or this privacy notice, contact support at darian.sepe@student.hnu.de.
